TASK 2: THE BOX COLLECTION TASK

In this task, you will see a grid containing 100 boxes.

  • Earnings: Each box you collect is worth $0.20.
  • The Rule: Out of the 100 boxes, exactly one box contains a hidden "Bomb." The location of the bomb is randomly determined by the computer and is completely unknown to you.
  • How to play: Once you click "Start," the computer will automatically collect one box every 0.5 seconds. You can click the "Stop" button at any time to lock in your collected boxes.
Payoff Mechanism:
  • If you click "Stop" before the bomb is collected, you keep the earnings for all the boxes you collected (Number of boxes × $0.20).
  • If the bomb is among the boxes you collected, your earnings for this task will drop to $0.
Examples for Clarification:
  • Example A: You click "Stop" after collecting 30 boxes. The bomb was hidden in box 45. Since 45 is greater than 30, you did not collect the bomb.
    Payoff: 30 boxes × $0.20 = $6.00.
  • Example B: You click "Stop" after collecting 60 boxes. The bomb was hidden in box 45. Since you collected the bomb...
    Payoff: $0.

(You will now complete a short quiz on your screen to ensure you perfectly understand the payout rules before the task begins).
