TASK 2: THE BOX COLLECTION TASK

You will see a grid of 100 boxes.

Earnings: Each box you collect is worth $0.20.

The Rule: Exactly one box contains a hidden "Bomb." Its location is randomly determined and unknown to you.

How to play: Click "Start" to begin. The computer automatically collects one box every 0.5 seconds. Click "Stop" at any time to lock in your collected boxes.

Payoff Mechanism:
If you click "Stop" before the bomb is collected, you keep the earnings ($Boxes x $0.20).
If you collect the bomb, your earnings for this task will be $0.

Examples for Clarification:

  • Example A: You stop at 30 boxes. The bomb was in box 45. You did not hit the bomb. Payoff: 30 boxes x $0.20 = $6.00.
  • Example B: You stop at 60 boxes. The bomb was in box 45. You hit the bomb. Payoff: $0.
