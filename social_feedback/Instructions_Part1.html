Participant Instructions

WELCOME & SEATING PROTOCOL

Welcome to this experiment. You are asked to participate in a study regarding economic decision-making. Upon entering the laboratory, you have been assigned to a computer station corresponding to a unique number. Please do not change seats or look at other screens. Turn off your mobile phones and do not communicate with other participants. If you have a question at any time, please raise your hand, and an experimenter will assist you privately. All your decisions and responses today will remain confidential and will only be reported in aggregate form.

PAYMENT (Random Lottery Incentive)

While everyone will complete the tasks, ONE participant in this room will be randomly selected at the end of the session by a computer draw. If you are selected, your final payout will be paid in cash based on the specific decisions you make in Task 2 today. Therefore, since your potential earnings depend on your choices, you should treat every decision as if it involves real money.

TASK 1: PROFILE CREATION & COMMUNITY INTERACTION

  1. Create your Profile: You will be presented with a gallery of visual stickers and text phrases. Please select exactly 3 stickers and 1 phrase that best represent your personal style or current mood. Your profile will be displayed anonymously; no names or identifying information will be shown to other participants.
  2. Profile Interaction: Once all profiles are created, your profile will be public to the room. The system will randomly display profiles from other participants, and you can choose to give a "Like" to the profiles you are attracted to or find appealing. Other participants will also be viewing the profiles.
  3. Transition: Once this phase concludes, the system will process the "Likes", and you will advance to Task 2.
