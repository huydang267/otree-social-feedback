Participant Instructions

WELCOME & SEATING PROTOCOL

Welcome to this experiment. You are asked to participate in a study regarding economic decision-making. Upon entering the laboratory, you have been assigned to a computer station corresponding to a unique number. Please do not change seats or look at other screens. Turn off your mobile phones and do not communicate with other participants. If you have a question at any time, raise your hand, and an experimenter will assist you privately. All your decisions will remain confidential and will only be reported in aggregate form.

PAYMENT (Random Lottery Incentive)

While everyone will complete the tasks, ONE participant in this room will be randomly selected at the end of the session by a computer draw. If you are selected, you will be paid in cash based on the specific decisions you make in Task 2 today. Therefore, you should treat every decision as if it involves real money.

TASK 1: PROFILE CREATION & PEER EVALUATION

Create your Profile: Select exactly 3 stickers and 1 phrase that best represent your personal style or current mood. Your profile will be displayed anonymously to others.

Peer Evaluation: You will view profiles from other participants and give a 'Like' to those you find appealing. Others will simultaneously evaluate your profile.

Transition: After the evaluation, the system will process the feedback, and you will advance to Task 2.
